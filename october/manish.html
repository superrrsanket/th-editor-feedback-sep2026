Sanket's review
GoodHis English was clean, and the font and color comments were few.
FixDidn't use the CIK, a few unpolished, unbranded animations, and too many styling and detail comments.

Manish's first draft for Ari's video was a bit out of place, with too many comments on styling and the small details. The good part is that his English was good enough that there weren't many language comments, and the font and color comments were few and manageable. The bigger problem was that he tried to innovate so much on his own that he didn't use the CIK at all. That was a waste of time, because there's no need to reinvent what already works. A few animations looked very out of place. They weren't polished or branded, and they didn't feel thoughtful, intentional or designed. It felt like text was just stuck onto the screen. That's fixable, and I understand it's his first video. Looking at it as a first video, it's still good enough. I'd rate it 6 out of 10.
