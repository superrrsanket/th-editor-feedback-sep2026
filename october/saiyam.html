GoodStrong concepts, and the CIK was used well. He started on his own and sent it early.
FixToo much on screen (animated icons), and the rough-edges effect was used because the kit has it, not because the video needed it.

Really good video overall. The concepts were really good, and the way Saiyam used the creator identity kit (CIK) was awesome. Two things to improve. First, in some places there was information overload, with animated icons and too much on the screen at once. Second, the rough edges effect was a bit distracting. The timeline was tight and we kept changing the CIK, and I appreciate that Saiyam didn't wait for any of that and started on his own. He even sent the video early, which was awesome.
