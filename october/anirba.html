Sanket's review
GoodA new style and he picked it up well. All the animations look good.
FixA digital style that didn't fit the client, quote-mark alignment and spacing, and the AI B-roll.

Really good video. It was a new style for him and he picked it up really well, and all the animations look good. Three things to improve. First, in some places he used a different style, a very digital one, that didn't go with this video. I've given this feedback in earlier rounds too: he sometimes confuses the style we want for the client. Second, basic things like left-aligning with the quote mark and the spacing could have been better. Third, the AI B-roll could have been a lot, lot better. Compared with what we do for James, the AI videos for Nigel can be much better. In short, the styling, the minor details and the AI videos can all be improved.
