Sanket's Review: Really well edited, and better than his usual work, so Innovative. I liked that he tried new kinds of animations, and there were very few minor errors. Two things to improve. First, the thinking behind the animations: some were added where they weren't needed, with no real reason for them. Second, how he spends his time, which matters because this video was delayed. Effort should go to the things that make a big difference, not a small one. Transitions between animations, like going from a cut to full screen, don't move the needle much, and a jump cut often looks better than a lot of motion that distracts and confuses. If we focus on what makes the video 80% better instead of 20% better, it would be awesome. Overall I really like the designs and the animation concepts. One animation didn't match what Vishen was saying, the one about being invisible and standing out.
