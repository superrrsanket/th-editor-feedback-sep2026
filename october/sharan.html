Sanket's review
GoodSimple, clean animations. He didn't overcomplicate it.
FixSupers jumping between lines, shadows, title vs sentence case, and more pattern interruptors.

I like the first draft: the animations were good, and I especially liked that he didn't overcomplicate it. Any editor could have piled on fancy animation here, and he stuck with simple, clean ones. A few minor things to fix: the supers didn't feel quite right, probably from context switching between lines. A super that jumps between separate lines looks abrupt, and one line reads much cleaner. Shadows and sentence case could have been handled better, and there was some title case vs sentence case confusion, with title case in the quote and in the pointers where we normally use sentence case. There were also fewer pattern interruptors than I'd like, so we can add more through the video and at the end.

V2 (Oct 12): only minor comments left, and I liked that he added extra pattern interruptors.
