Oct 13Approved

Sanket's Review: I like the first draft: the animations were good, and I especially liked that he didn't overcomplicate it. Any editor could have piled on fancy animation here, and he stuck with simple, clean ones. A few minor things to fix: the supers didn't feel quite right, probably from context switching between lines. A super that jumps between separate lines looks abrupt, and one line reads much cleaner. Shadows and sentence case could have been handled better, and there was some title case vs sentence case confusion, with title case in the quote and in the pointers where we normally use sentence case. There were also fewer pattern interruptors than I'd like, so we can add more through the video and at the end.

V2 (Oct 12): only minor comments left, and I liked that he added extra pattern interruptors.

Sanket's Review: V1 sent Oct 20. Overall clean, but there were a lot of spacing issues that could have been caught while editing, before sending. Zoom-ins and zoom-outs were few or very subtle. Subtle is good, but we also need to use zooms and jump cuts to hide cuts and to emphasize sentences, and that was lacking. There was too much information and the content could have been more refined. For example, the hook had one graphic packed with information, and a hook should lean on visuals more than information. I really loved the AI heroes: they looked realistic, and he kept the character consistent. The colors also looked very different from the CIK, which I'll ask him about. There were a lot of minor mistakes that could have been fixed easily.
