Sanket's Review: V1 (Oct 6): Missed opportunity. The whole video felt plain and had room for a lot more pattern interruptors, and the hook was slow. The sticky notes were a good idea, but the grammar in them needed another pass, sources were missing, and the research papers weren't shown at all. Waiting on assets from Vishen's team explains part of it, but not why the video felt this empty throughout.

V2 (week 2): Good enough, and most of the V1 comments were fixed, but we could have added more. This video wasn't graphic or animation heavy, so the better move was to make the graphics we did create better. Attention to detail was missing. Showing the research papers is the bare minimum, and highlighting the relevant passages is the extra 10%. The scrolling on the website was so fast that nobody could read anything, which is a thought-process problem. The last newspaper report was just a pasted screenshot, and a digital screen overlay would have made it look far higher quality.
