Patterns Claude flagged
01Motion (24) is the largest category, split almost evenly between the two videos; Content & accuracy (13) and Typography (12) follow.
02"Switch white background to beige" repeats 3 times within the same V1 round on 3 Keys to Manifesting.
03Headspace inconsistency is flagged 3 separate times across one video (3 Keys to Manifesting), all in the same V1 round.
04Both videos this month were tag-teamed or coordinated differently than a solo edit: $0 MBA was split with Lokesh; 3 Keys had a specific CapCut preset step requested before the A-cut was finalized.
05$0 MBA has no verdict on file (predates the review workflow); 3 Keys to Manifesting is the only one of Anirba's 2 videos with one.
Action points — heading into October
01Positioning and composition of the cut — the most important note this cycle. Zoom in a little more and keep the subject centered; get this right at the V1 stage, since a change here later means redoing every zoom in the video.
02Reduce repetitive animations — keep experimenting with new animation styles rather than reusing the same ones.
03Keep client styling separated — some Ryan Diaz-style elements have been showing up in Vishen edits. Worth thinking through which visual elements belong to which client before applying them.
04Smoothness — same as the wider team ask: frame glitches and title case need tightening up.
05More graphics — bump up the frequency and intensity of full-screen graphics and pattern interrupters.
What's working: strong instincts on content — flags what to trim and offers real opinions on the material, which is genuinely useful. Still the best fit on the team for Ryan Diaz videos.
